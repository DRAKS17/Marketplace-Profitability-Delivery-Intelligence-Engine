## 4. The 'Late GMV = Lost Revenue' Myth vs True Commercial Exposure

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **EXECUTIVE FACT CHECK**: When an order arrives late, the customer did **not** automatically cancel or refund it. The product was delivered and retained by the buyer.

- **Total GMV Associated with Late Deliveries**: **$1,163,699.21** (8.76% of all delivered GMV).
- **Gross Platform Commission Earned on These Late Orders**: **$139,643.91**.
- **Direct Late Voucher Remedy Cost ($15 / late order)**: **-$117,975.00**.
- **Direct Customer Service Complaint Overhead ($5 / ticket)**: **-$11,510.00**.
- **Total Direct Operational Exposure**: **$129,485.00**.
- **Net Cash Retained by Platform on Late Deliveries**: **+$10,158.91**.

### Conclusion on Late Delivery Economics:
Late deliveries do **not** cost the platform $1.16 Million BRL in lost gross merchandise value. The true direct cost to the marketplace is **$129,485.00 BRL** in customer service overhead and goodwill compensation credits.
The primary commercial damage of delivery failure is **not immediate transaction loss**, but **long-term brand erosion** (CSAT collapsing from 4.29 to 2.57 stars) and the resulting suppression of repeat customer lifetime value.
